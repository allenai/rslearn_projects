Labels are written to the JSONL on weka as each key is pressed — nothing is held in the browser. Cards keep their place on the page after you label them; the queue is rebuilt when you change page or filter.

auto stretch rescales each crop to its own 2–98% DN range; production stretch is the fixed (DN−5000)×255/12000 window the deployed pipeline draws, i.e. what the model's own crop looks like. Low-sun and dark-water scenes read as near-black in the production stretch even where the bands separate cleanly — judge on auto, and check production when the call depends on brightness.
